## Current milestone
Saved local input layouts completed and validated on proto/punchies/saved-input-layouts from main 9ce9087. Ready for the live merge and normal WIP deployment.

## What was implemented
Settings > Input Setup > Local Inputs offers Touch, Keyboard 1/2 and Controller 1/2 editors. Save commits the selected profile; Cancel discards it; Reset defaults stages a reset. Touch buttons and the joystick hint can be dragged in a preview; Jab/Cross move together. Keyboard bindings use rounded keycaps, and controller bindings use Xbox labels and face-button colours. Runtime controls and tutorial prompts read saved bindings immediately, including after resuming a paused fight. Combat rules pass (owner approved): each punch now has counterStartup and counterRecovery flags in tune (jab 0/0, cross 1/1, hook 1/1, uppercut 0/1); post-dodge, guard-release and dodge-tail still counter with any punch. Exhausted fighters can guard (no perfect guard window, free, chipped by jab/cross/hook at guard.exhaustedChipMult 0.5, uppercut still ignores guard) and dodge at dodge.exhaustedEfficacy 0.5 of the invincible frames and distance with no stamina cost; they keep half-damage punches. New test-punchies-states.mjs; states, emergency and geometry tests added to the deploy workflow. Hit and block stun (owner approved): per-punch hitStun, blockStun and sourStun frames in tune, scaled per character by the push multiplier, lock the defender (no actions, no walking) and replace hit.pushLockFrames; counters multiply the hit stun by hit.counterStunMult (1.5); guard is held through block stun without a release penalty. Resulting frame advantage (attacker, sweet hit / block / sour hit): jab +3/-1/-3, cross +2/-6/-8, hook +2/-3/-5, uppercut +5. Stun tuning (owner approved after a stun-lock report): base hit stun trimmed 2 to 3 frames (jab 10, cross 23, hook 14, uppercut 20), hit.counterStunMult 1.5 to 1.2 so a counter widens the window without guaranteeing a jab follow-up, and locked fighters can walk at movement.lockMoveMult (0.6, hit lock only; blockers use the guard multiplier). Resulting attacker advantage (sweet hit / block / sour hit): jab +1/-1/-3, cross 0/-6/-8, hook 0/-3/-5, uppercut +2; counters +3 to +6.

## Key technical decisions
Extended the registered punchies:localInputs:v1 save without breaking device assignments or old saves. Profiles remain independent. Physical keyboard codes, duplicate rejection, selected-controller rising edges, trigger threshold, held-input suppression and fixed menu navigation prevent capture leakage. Touch positions are normalized and constrained to safe areas without disc overlap; preview, runtime drawing, icons, labels and hit testing share geometry. The joystick retains its tuned radius and floating behaviour. No combat, tune, art, dependency or networking changes.

## Open questions
None for the requested scope. A non-exhausted fighter with 0 to 17 stamina cannot dodge while an exhausted one can (half efficacy), an inversion to review after playtest. Bot and AI logic do not yet use exhausted guard or dodge. Guard and dodge exposed-window values (24 frames each) are still under discussion. Animation and effect suggestions for every fighter state are with Codex pending owner permission. Stun values are first-pass and need playtest tuning; bot and AI do not yet read the lock, so they may queue actions into it (buffered, harmless). Whether a locked defender should be able to buffer a dodge out of stun is currently yes (6-frame buffer).
