# Modern / Classic integrated contract

New match preferences default to Modern and persist independently for P1/P2 in the registered local-input key. Device/layout edits preserve those fields and all Classic bindings. Modern Normal uses the saved Jab binding as a held input; Super uses the existing Super binding. Solo/online players use their local P1 preference; local versus uses each side's preference. First play explicitly chooses Modern even with an absent or Classic preference. Tutorial path selection is a scene-local override and does not change match preferences.

Modern has movement, held Normal Attack and the existing charged Super. At legal action start, choose Hook within its actual scaled fist reach; otherwise Cross strictly beyond Jab reach and within Cross reach; otherwise Jab. Collider envelopes do not extend these selection thresholds. Existing collision geometry still determines hit quality. The choice remains committed through the punch. Hold repeats at the existing full startup/active/recovery/whiff/fatigue cadence.

All outgoing HP damage, including guard chip, receives one 0.8 multiplier in the common contact resolver. Actual CodeX follow-ups, Buffalo follow-up, Wave projectile, Eagle and Rising Upper tests verify the same factor. Fatigue, counter, sweet/sour, dodge buff and Super multipliers remain. Stun, push and hitstop are unchanged. Modern never spends/drains/recovers stamina, enters emergency recovery or receives its damage/defense penalties. Its canonical meter is full/inert and its HUD side hides STM.

Neutral input raises legal High Guard automatically and can never Perfect Guard, including stale/manual guard inputs. Movement/attack lower guard under existing rules. Modern stick hysteresis enters neutral at 0.12 and leaves at 0.20. Its raw normalized analog input bypasses the old device deadzones, which remain unchanged for Classic. A neutral-to-direction transition latches direction and arms twelve active combat frames, freezing through hitstop/intro. Movement alone never dodges. A real ordinary/Eagle/CodeX/Wave collision candidate triggers one legal dodge before damage; the window is consumed once. Existing dodge invulnerability, penalty, retrigger restriction and normal stun legality apply. New core normalization uses Math.sqrt for deterministic replay.

Online HELLO/PAIRED include each side's selected mode. START requires the agreed pair and its checksum; a host cannot replace the peer's choice. The new simulation rules cohort rejects old clients. Rollback packs held Normal separately and predicts it as held input; modes/stick state live in snapshots and replay hashes. A whole BO3 retains its pair. Settings changes can be proposed only after whole-set completion for rematch/reselection; an early peer proposal is deferred until local completion. They never alter an active fighter. Disconnect/terminal ownership fixes remain intact.

All playable scenes bind canonical modes before the first sampled frame. AI/training/tutorial opponents retain Classic scripted behavior. Survival records the run's chosen mode and resets its dodge window between waves. Older checkpoints hydrate only missing simulation fields as Classic in memory, retaining existing saves and best records without an automatic rewrite or reset.

First-play Bruno uses a bounded per-instance HP multiplier of 1.25 (120 to 150 at the current tune); the configurable cap is 1.5. Other Bruno/roster/AI HP is unchanged. The historical six-hit forced drain is removed so HP and actual damage determine KO. The existing low-aggression timing and player safety floor remain. Its Normal/Super pair matches regular Modern controls. Modern and Classic tutorials have separate progress; legacy progress belongs to Classic. Modern lessons omit manual punch/guard/dodge and stamina controls, teaching context, guard, dodge, fatigue, Super, hit quality and stun instead.
