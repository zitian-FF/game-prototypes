# Publish the Muse project

Publish the downloaded Myuse Godot 4.3 procedural animation demo on the existing GitHub Pages site at `/game-prototypes/myuse/`. Preserve its automatic idle, run, mid-air spin, land loop and original timings. It plays hands-free on desktop and phone.

This user-requested Godot import is an exception to the repository's Phaser stack. Keep it isolated from the Phaser prototypes and their build. Unused bonus artwork and original captures remain in the downloaded local archive; no image assets are needed by this procedural rig.

Out of scope: redesigning the character, new gameplay, replacing artwork, and changing other prototypes or hosting destinations.
